About me

I trained as a dentist, and I still think like a clinician: start with the user, respect the workflow, question the assumption.

Dentistry gave me my foundation, but what pulled me in was the technology around care: how software can make a clinic run better, and a diagnosis come faster. That curiosity took me from the chair to building health products.

At Ve-Lyra Labs, I sit between clinicians, operations, and engineers, turning what doctors actually need into something a team can build and test.

Healthtech is where my heart is, but the way I work applies anywhere: I ground decisions in evidence, listen closely to users, and keep cross-functional teams moving toward real outcomes.
